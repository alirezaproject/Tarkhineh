# tarkhineh

Tarkhineh is a cross-platform mobile application built with Flutter for Android and iOS, designed to provide a seamless and engaging experience for food lovers.  It connects users to a rich menu of traditional and modern meals, offers personalized deals, and allows them to easily browse, order, and rate their meals right from their phone.
